# baseline.py reads this file. These match the README taxonomy.

analysis: makes an argument backed by a specific checkable fact, such as a rate, a counting stat, a slash line, a dollar figure, or a series result
hot_take: states a confident claim and offers no checkable fact to support it
reaction: responds to a moment that just happened, a feeling or a box score, and does not argue a claim
